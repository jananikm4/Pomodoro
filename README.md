# 🥀 Study Ritual.

> *"I ain't never had a doubt inside me • And if I ever told you that I did, I'm fuckin' lyin'"*

An aesthetic, immersive, and highly atmosphere-driven productivity workspace built with Streamlit. 

Most out-of-the-box study timers are clinical, sterile, and corporate. They treat productivity like a spreadsheet. **Study Ritual** rejects that. Built out of a frustration with tools that felt completely uninspiring, this app transforms standard time-blocking into an atmospheric ritual tailored for late-night grinds, empire-building mindsets, and deep focus flows.

---

## ✨ Features That Make It Different

* **🎨 Ambient Space Registries:** Instantly pivot your environment across over 20+ custom-tuned, high-contrast dark and neon aesthetics (from `🏺 Gilded Bistre` to `👑 Regal Velvet Navy`).
* **🩺 Real-Time Heartbeat Engine:** Built utilizing seamless dynamic background autorefreshing to ensure your layout streams smoothly without falling asleep when you change tabs.
* **🎯 In-Workspace Objectives:** An integrated, session-persistent target box that saves instantly (`Ctrl + Enter`) and survives timer state resets and ticking cycles.
* **🔮 Dynamic Stream Headers:** Your browser tab title updates natively to show your exact progress block or remaining break time at a quick glance.
* **⏳ Automated Strategy Projections:** Streamlined triggers that inject curated motivational philosophies and structural rest activities right when your mind starts drifting.
